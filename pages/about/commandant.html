From the Commandant

Guardians of Tradition

We are the guardians of tradition. We understand the gravity of the ceremonies we train for, honoring our nation with every precise movement and respectful flag display.

Our legacy is built on humility, respect, and dignity. Civil Air Patrol Honor Guardsmen embody these values alongside integrity, volunteer service, excellence, and respect. They are driven servant leaders, dedicated to maintaining the highest standards and demonstrating exceptional self-awareness and discipline in all they do.

CAP Honor Guardsmen fulfill a vital role as representatives of the Civil Air Patrol, the U.S. Air Force Auxiliary. This publication equips them with the knowledge to ensure the standardized execution of all CAP Honor Guard ceremonies and duties. It covers essential topics like drill manuals, movements, uniforms, and various ceremony types.

WILLIAM JACKSON, SMSgt, CAP
Former Commandant, Mid-Atlantic Region Honor Guard Academy

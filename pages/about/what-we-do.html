What We Do

Civil Air Patrol Honor Guards render dynamic service to the unit, the community, and the nation. Functioning as premier civic ambassadors, our contingents execute precision ceremonial sequences across three foundational mission elements:

Colors Element

Tasked with the presentation, posting, and retrieval of National, State, and Civil Air Patrol Colors during civic gatherings, formal banquets, sporting events, parades, and official ceremonies.


Ceremonial Element

Coordinates specialized ceremonial functions that require solemn dignity and military decorum, including:

  • Change of Command & Retirement Ceremonies
  • Awards Banquets & Milestone Promotions
  • POW/MIA Missing Man Table Presentations
  • Official Flag Retirement & Dignified Disposal Ceremonies
  • Wreath-laying Honors, Monument Unveilings, and Cordon Escorts

Funeral Element

Renders final honors to deceased CAP members upon family request in accordance with CAPP 3, Chapter 8. Duties include casket/urn watch, remains escort, and dignified flag-folding details.

Safety & Operational Boundaries: Cadets are strictly prohibited from acting as pallbearers carrying an occupied casket, and units are prohibited from operating firing parties.

Who We Are

The Ceremonial Guardsman

A Civil Air Patrol Honor Guardsman is an individual trained in CAP Honor Guard philosophy, ceremony, uniform wear, and authorized publications. We execute precise ceremonial drill and wear an authorized distinct uniform in the performance of official CAP ambassadorial duties.


Our Philosophy

CAP Honor Guardsmen exhibit exceptional professionalism, immaculate appearance, and rigorous conduct. We serve with humility, respect, and dignity, representing Civil Air Patrol and the United States Air Force Auxiliary above all else.

Membership is a privilege earned through dedicated volunteer service, character development, and rigorous self-discipline. While not an elite exclusionary club, it demands unwavering commitment to excellence from every cadet and adult leader.


The Honor Guard Creed

I am a proud member of a Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard.
My standards of conduct and high level of professionalism show the respect for and dedication to my craft and those with whom I serve.
I have earned the right to wear the ceremonial uniform, which is steeped in rich tradition and history.
I am superbly conditioned to perfect all movements in every drill or ceremony.
Neither the type of ceremony, severity of temperature, nor size of the audience will ever dictate the level of quality of my performance.
I am constantly driven to excel by a strong sense of dedication, patriotism, love for my fellow man, God, Country, and Civil Air Patrol.
While performing, I stand sharp and crisp; motionless by choice, for I have voluntarily chosen to represent every member, past and present, of the United States Air Force Auxiliary – Civil Air Patrol.
I am a Ceremonial Guardsman.

Motto: "Honor Guard - Not to set the standards, but to exceed them!"

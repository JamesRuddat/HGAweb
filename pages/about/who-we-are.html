Who We Are


The Ceremonial Guardsman

A Civil Air Patrol Honor Guardsman is an individual trained in CAP Honor Guard philosophy, ceremony, uniform wear, and authorized publications. We execute precise ceremonial drill and wear an authorized distinct uniform in the performance of official CAP ambassadorial duties.


Our Philosophy

CAP Honor Guardsmen exhibit exceptional professionalism, immaculate appearance, and rigorous conduct. We serve with humility, respect, and dignity, representing Civil Air Patrol and the United States Air Force Auxiliary above all else.

Membership is a privilege earned through dedicated volunteer service, character development, and rigorous self-discipline. While not an elite exclusionary club, it demands unwavering commitment to excellence from every cadet and adult leader.

Motto

Honor Guard - Not to set the standards, but to exceed them!

Color Guard

Color guards represent the heartbeat of squadron and group-level ceremonies. Composed of four cadets (two flag bearers and two rifle guards), color guards provide essential ceremonial training and represent Civil Air Patrol at community parades, school assemblies, sporting events, and cadet competitions.

Governing Doctrine

Unlike Honor Guards, color guards strictly adhere to standard Air Force and CAP drill publications:

  • CAPP 60-33: Civil Air Patrol Drill and Ceremonies (Chapter 7)
  • DAFPAM 34-1203: Air Force Drill and Ceremonies
  • TC 3-21.5: U.S. Army Drill and Ceremonies (for weapon manual-of-arms)

Operational Scope

Color guards are appropriate for general CAP ceremonies held at the squadron and group level (e.g., encampment graduations, squadron changes of command). When civic parades celebrate events like the 4th of July, Memorial Day, or local founder anniversaries, a well-trained color guard is the prescribed asset.

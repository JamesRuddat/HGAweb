Honor Guard Academy

Mission

The Honor Guard Academy prepares cadets to serve on unit color guards and honor guards while instilling lifelong character, self-awareness, and personal discipline. Academies operate with Region Commander approval and provide a minimum of 42 instructional contact hours over a 6 to 7-day curriculum.

Curriculum Blocks

  • Leadership Block: Mentorship, staff meetings, and command presence.
  • Drill Block: Ceremonial manual-of-arms, slow cadence, flag bearing, and synchronization.
  • Fitness Block: Physical stamina required for motionless casket watch and long ceremony duty.
  • Character Block: Core values application, humility, and servant leadership.
  • Inspections: Daily barracks checks, uniform preparation, and spot-inspection evaluations.

The Chief's Challenge

The culminating evaluation of the Academy is the Chief's Challenge—a multi-event evaluation of randomly selected cadet teams demonstrating real-world ceremony execution under pressure:

  • Indoor Posting of Colors: Navigating narrow aisles and dias podiums to post the colors.
  • Outdoor Reveille & Retreat: Hoisting to half-staff and executing 4-person lowering and flag fold.
  • Standard Drill: 50' x 90' field routine evaluating sequence, timing, snap, and command voice.
  • Uniform Prep & Inspection: Building and pressing a regulation uniform within 30 minutes.
  • Academic Exam: 50-question closed-book exam covering leadership, aerospace, and drill doctrine.

Graduation & Awards

Cadets earn full graduation credit by completing at least 80% (34 contact hours) of instruction and demonstrating satisfactory adherence to CAP core values. Graduates are authorized the prestigious silver shoulder cord (Vanguard) worn on the left shoulder of the service coat and blue shirt per CAPR 39-1 §9.3.3.

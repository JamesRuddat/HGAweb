Honor Guard

Program Overview

Civil Air Patrol unit Honor Guards provide formalized ceremonial support for wing and region functions, VIP arrivals, formal mess dress banquets, and funeral ceremonies honoring deceased members.

Authorized Distinctive Uniforms (CAPR 39-1 §9.6)

Uniform Class Accouterments & Distinctives Authorized Venues
Class A Service Dress Service coat, ascot with HG patch, silver shoulder cord, service cap with chinstrap down, white gloves, white pistol belt, tapped low quarters. Solemn ceremonies, funeral details, formal events, and wing/region conferences.
Class B Blue Service Short/long-sleeve blue shirt, silver shoulder cord, service cap, white gloves, white pistol belt, tapped low quarters. Informal civic gatherings and outdoor sporting colors presentations.

Strict Restrictions

  • Weapons: Bayonets, swords, sabers, and operational weapons are strictly banned. Only deactivated or replica parade rifles are authorized. Firing parties are strictly prohibited.
  • Pallbearing: Guardsmen are strictly prohibited from acting as pallbearers to carry a casket. Remnant/urn escorts and flag-folding details are authorized under adult OIC supervision.
  • First Sergeant Diamonds: Per CAPR 39-1 §10.10.4, diamond insignia may only be worn while assigned as the active Honor Guard First Sergeant.

Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard Uniform

In accordance with CAPR 39-1, Civil Air Patrol Uniform Regulation, Section 9.6, only trained Civil Air Patrol Honor Guardsmen are authorized to wear the Honor Guard distinctive uniform, and strictly while performing or traveling directly to and from official CAP Honor Guard duties.

Unit Uniformity: All CAP Honor Guardsmen performing together at an event—regardless of element or contingent assignment—must wear the exact same uniform combination (same class, sleeve length, and tie/tab configuration).

Authorized Uniform Combinations (CAPR 39-1 §9.6)

CAPR 39-1 authorizes two ceremonial combinations for cadet and senior CAP Honor Guardsmen, dictated by the formality of the venue and the prescribed attire of the official party:

Service Dress Honor Guard Uniform (Class A – CAPR 39-1 §9.6.5)

The preferred and most formal combination, required for funerals, memorial services, wreaths, and formal events where guests wear mess dress or semi-formal attire. Consists of the standard Class A service dress coat and trousers/slacks paired with CAP Honor Guard distinctives:

  • Ceremonial Ascot: Worn under the open-collar blue shirt (top two to three buttons undone, collar tucked under) over a plain white V-neck t-shirt. The embroidered blue-and-red CAP Honor Guard Shield patch is centered on the ascot.
  • Service Coat Blousing: Must appear well-fitted, wrinkle-free, and smoothly bloused beneath the pistol belt without bunching.

Blue Service Honor Guard Uniform (Class B – CAPR 39-1 §9.6.6)

Authorized for Colors Element performances at informal civic gatherings or outdoor sporting events where the official party wears casual or utility attire. Consists of the short- or long-sleeve blue service shirt with CAP Honor Guard distinctives (tie/tab optional on short sleeves; female blouses remain tucked at all times). Never wear Class B for funerals or formal ceremonies due to heat alone.

Distinctive Accouterments & Placement

Uniform Item CAPR 39-1 & Program Specifications Critical Wear & Safety Rules
Service Cap Male wheel-style service cap with Cadet Officer/Senior cap device (wings on buttons pointing up) and rear chinstrap. Two-finger gap between visor and bridge of nose; visor never covers eyes. Chinstrap worn down during performances; flush/up at all other times.
White Pistol Belt White web belt with chrome-finish buckle, eyelets, and two chrome keepers spaced two finger-widths from buckle. Class A: Centered between 2nd and 3rd coat buttons. Class B: Covers blue trouser belt. Omitted during flag-folding details to prevent coat bunching.
Silver Shoulder Cord Box-braid silver cord worn on the left shoulder seam. Authorized for CAP Honor Guardsmen only; senior members do not wear the silver shoulder cord (CAPR 60-1 §8.4.6.2).
White Parade Gloves Plain white tight-fitting parade gloves ending in a clean edge just past the wrist (fold under if needed). Must remain stain-free; never use gloves to wipe chrome belt buckles (use a microfiber cloth).
Tapped Low Quarters High-gloss or poromeric low quarters equipped with metal heel taps and toe cheaters; edges dressed black. Laces tucked inside shoes. Never wear tapped shoes to normal unit meetings or non-Honor Guard events.
1st Sgt Diamond Cadet First Sergeant diamond insignia per CAPR 39-1 §10.10.4. Worn only while actively assigned and serving as the CAP Honor Guard First Sergeant.

Grooming, Jewelry & Wear Restrictions

  • Hair & Grooming: Male members must be freshly clean-shaven. Female members with long hair must secure it in a neat bun at the nape of the neck using hair-colored pins/nets (no ponytails, braids hanging over the collar, or fabric scrunchies). Short hair must be pinned behind the ears without protruding under the service cap.
  • Nails & Cosmetics: Fingernails trimmed to no more than 1/4-inch past the fingertip; only clear polish or white-tipped French manicure is authorized. Cosmetics (females only) must blend naturally without drawing attention.
  • Jewelry Policy: Rings, bracelets, earrings, and watches are prohibited. Exception: The Cadet Honor Guard Commander may wear a plain watch on the left wrist for time checks so it remains concealed during salutes. Wallets, cell phones, purses, and transition lenses are not carried during performances.
  • Travel & Partial Uniforms: Members must change out of the ceremonial uniform immediately following an event. Partial uniforms (e.g., wearing an ascot without the service coat) are strictly forbidden outside a vehicle or changing room.

Honor Guard Emblems & Heraldry

Official heraldic insignia, seals, and shields of the Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard and Honor Guard Academy. These assets are authorized for use in ceremony programs, certificates, educational manuals, and uniform ascot patches per CAPR 900-2 and CAPR 39-1 §9.6.

Title

To preserve organizational identity, maintain heraldic uniformity, and prevent confusion with sister-service components specifically the United States Air Force Honor Guard (USAF Honor Guard) and United States Space Force Honor Guard (USSF Honor Guard) all publications, scripts, correspondence, and digital media must comply with official Civil Air Patrol brand guidelines:

Title Forms:
  • Always write the full entity title preceded by the complete organization name: Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard or Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard Academy.
  • Subsequent mentions may use CAP Honor Guard or the initialism HGA only after the full title has been spelled out first.
  • Subordinate Activity Entities: Follow the official format where Civil Air Patrol precedes the functional program name (e.g., Civil Air Patrol Mid-Atlantic Region Honor Guard Academy). For web metadata or search optimization, reversing with a comma is permitted (e.g., Mid-Atlantic Region Honor Guard Academy, Civil Air Patrol).

Capitalization & Style

  • The terms “Honor Guard” and “Honor Guard Academy” are official proper titles and must always be capitalized. Writing “honor guard” in lowercase when referring to the program, academy, or unit contingent is prohibited.
  • Never introduce the abbreviation HGA without first spelling out “Honor Guard Academy” in the same section or document.
  • HGA must appear in all capital letters without punctuation (never “Hga” or “H.G.A.”).
  • When spoken during ceremonies, narrations, or verbal presentations, “Honor Guard Academy” must be articulated in full, never spoken phonetically as the single word “hga.”

Heraldic Application Matrix

Guidelines for selecting the correct format based on publication media and ceremonial context:

Variant Colors Authorized Applications & Wear Rules
CAP Blue Shield Blue & Red Primary Program Emblem. Official insignia of the Unit Honor Guard program. Authorized as the embroidered uniform patch worn centered on the ceremonial ascot with the Honor Guard Uniform.
CAP Blue Seal Blue & Red Primary Academy Seal. Official mark of the Honor Guard Academy (HGA). Authorized for uniform wear exclusively by graduates of an accredited HGA, official graduation programs, and academy credentials.
Air Force Style Shield Full Color (AF Heritage) Limited Scope. Reference use only for historical displays, joint AF ceremonial guides, and select presentation items. Not approved for uniform wear or ascots.
Air Force Style Seal Full Color (AF Heritage) Limited Scope. Reference and commemorative display only. Not approved for uniform wear.
Monochrome Shield Black & White Single-Color Media Only. Restricted to single-color apparel (screen-printed PT/flight shirts), black-and-white field manuals, routine paperwork, and photocopied administrative forms.
Monochrome Seal Black & White Single-Color Media Only. Restricted to single-color academy handouts, exam covers, black-and-white printings, and single-ink merchandise.

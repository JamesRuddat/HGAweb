Honor Guard Emblems & Heraldry

Official heraldic insignia, seals, and shields of the Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard and Honor Guard Academy. These assets are authorized for use in ceremony programs, certificates, educational manuals, and uniform ascot patches per CAPR 900-2 and CAPR 39-1 §9.6.

1. Unit Honor Guard Shields

The primary shield emblem represents the unit honor guard program and ceremonial elements. It is authorized for wear on the ceremonial ascot with the Service Dress Honor Guard Uniform (Class A).

3. Authorized Heraldic Application Matrix

Guidelines for selecting the correct format based on publication media and ceremonial context:

Insignia Variant Color Profile Authorized Applications Governing Policy
Air Force Style Shield Full Color (AF Heritage) Formal joint-service programs, presentation binders, website hero banners DAFMAN 34-165
CAP Blue Shield CAP Navy & Red Ascot embroidery patch, unit guidons, official awards programs CAPR 39-1 §9.6.7.8
Monochrome Shield Black & White Field SOP manuals, photocopied inspection rubrics, black-and-white orders CAPR 900-2
Air Force Style Seal Full Color (AF Heritage) Honor Guard Academy diplomas, challenge coin minting, ceremonial dais banners CAPP 3 Ch. 8
CAP Blue Seal CAP Navy & Red Academy graduation programs, student dossiers, regional cadet records CAPR 60-1 §8.4.6
Monochrome Seal Black & White Written exam covers, daily staff briefings, trainee sign-in rosters CAPP 60-71

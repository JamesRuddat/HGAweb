Civil Air Patrol Regulatory Library

All members of the CAP Honor Guard and Color Guard programs must adhere strictly to current National Headquarters regulations and curriculum pamphlets:

Regulations (Mandatory Compliance)

  • CAPR 39-1 – Civil Air Patrol Uniform Regulation: Defines grooming standards and wear specifications for the Class A & B Honor Guard Distinctive Uniforms (§9.6) and Cadet First Sergeant insignia (§10.10.4).
  • CAPR 60-1 – Cadet Program Management: Governs cadet eligibility, graduation requirements, silver shoulder cord authorizations (§8.4.6.2), and special activities.
  • CAPR 60-2 – Cadet Protection Policy: Safety and supervision standards; strictly prohibits bayonets, live weapons, and hazardous assignments.
  • CAPR 900-2 – Civil Air Patrol Name, Seal, Logo, and Flag Etiquette: Authoritative heraldry guide defining the display and precedence of national, regional, wing, and squadron flags.

Pamphlets (Training Guides)

  • CAPP 60-33 – Civil Air Patrol Drill and Ceremonies: Baseline manual for flight, squadron, and color guard regulation drill.
  • CAPP 3 – Guide to Civil Air Patrol Protocol (Chapter 8): Protocol rules for funeral and memorial services for deceased CAP members.
  • CAPP 60-71 – Cadet Encampment Handbook: Pocket resource for leadership, customs, and cadet knowledge.

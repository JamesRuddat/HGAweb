Drill Styles: Regulation vs. Ceremonial

Separation of Disciplines

A common error within cadet programs is the mixing of regulation drill with ceremonial drill. These two disciplines evolved from different doctrinal backgrounds and must remain distinct:

Characteristic Regulation Drill Ceremonial Drill
Governing Manual CAPP 60-33 / DAFPAM 34-1203 DAFMAN 34-165
Primary Users Color Guards, Encampments, Squadrons Honor Guard contingents & elements
Cadence Quick Time (100–120 steps/min) Slow Time (60 steps/min)
Position of Attention Heels together, 45-degree angle Toes and heels completely together
Mark Time Balls of feet 4 inches from ground Knees raised high, horizontal thigh angle
Footwear Standard shined low quarters/boots Low quarters with heel taps and cheaters
Important: Components of ceremonial drill (high mark time, slow cadence, heels/toes touching) are strictly prohibited from use by color guards and cadet competitions.

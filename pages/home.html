About the Creator & Project

This knowledge repository was developed out of a deep dedication to ceremonial excellence, standardization, and the continued professional development of cadets and adult leaders within the Civil Air Patrol Honor Guard and color guard communities.

About the Creator

As an active member and student of Civil Air Patrol drill and ceremonies doctrine, I built this resource to address a recurring challenge across squadrons and wings: accessible, consolidated, and unambiguous ceremonial training reference material.

From organizing uniform inspections and procuring authorized hardware to navigating the nuances between regulation quick-time marching and ceremonial movements, this platform brings together doctrinal guidelines into an interactive, structured study system that members can rely on from any device.

Why This Site Exists

Following the transition from the centralized National Honor Guard Academy to regional programs, unit-level training programs depend heavily on accessible best practices, consistent manuals, and clear study aids. This site exists to:

  • Bridge the Gap: Clarify the essential distinction between standard regulation color guard drill (CAPP 60-33 / DAFPAM 34-1203) and dedicated ceremonial Honor Guard drill (DAFMAN 34-165).
  • Centralize Procurement: Provide verified procurement guides, part numbers, and finish standards to ensure units invest in compliant, authorized equipment.
  • Foster Excellence: Equip cadets preparing for an Honor Guard Academy, cadet competition, or unit ceremonies with self-paced study guides and scripts.

Doctrinal Alignment & Status

All curriculum outlines, uniform guides, manual-of-arms sequences, and ceremonial scripts published on this site strictly follow official Civil Air Patrol regulations regarding drill, ceremonies, uniforms, and cadet protection, including CAPR 39-1, CAPR 60-1, CAPR 60-2, CAPR 900-2, and CAPP 60-33.

Important Notice on Status:
This website is an unofficial training resource and study guide. It is not an official publication of National Headquarters, the United States Air Force, or the United States Government. Content hosted here remains unofficial unless specifically authorized and approved for operational use by a CAP unit commander, wing commander, region commander, or National Headquarters. In any instance of conflict between this site and official publications, official Civil Air Patrol regulations take precedence.
